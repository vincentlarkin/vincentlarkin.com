Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

vincentlarkin.com is a personal static website. I do not run user accounts, comments, payments, advertising profiles, or marketing forms on this site.

Data Collected Automatically

Like most websites, the server and Cloudflare may process basic request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and security signals. This is used to deliver the site, protect it from abuse, and troubleshoot availability.

Cookies and Local Storage

The site uses browser local storage to remember your selected theme and language. Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish visits. Advertising and ad-personalization features are disabled.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand site use, including page views, navigation, approximate scroll depth, time actively engaged, gallery interactions, video progress, theme and language selections, contact-link clicks, downloads, and links to other websites.

Google may also process technical information needed to provide Analytics, such as device and browser details, referring page, approximate location derived from an IP address, and timestamps. I do not send names, email addresses, account identifiers, or form contents to Google Analytics, and this site does not use Analytics for advertising.

Third Parties

The site is served through Cloudflare. Some pages may request public GitHub API data in your browser to show site updates or profile information. Google processes Analytics data under its own Privacy Policy. External links, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, status pages, and archived projects, are governed by their own privacy policies.
