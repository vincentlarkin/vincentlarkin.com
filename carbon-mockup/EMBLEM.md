Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Reference image: Vincent Larkin's existing personal heraldic emblem. Create ONE simplified, Carbon-design-language rendition of this emblem for the black masthead of his personal website. Preserve the recognizable pelican at the top facing left, shield outline, central upright sword, and paired balanced scales, but reduce them to bold geometric shapes and clean, consistent pictogram strokes. Preserve the emblem's identity and its main arrangement; simplify tiny interior heraldic details for legibility at only 40 pixels tall. Style: IBM Carbon-compatible geometric pictogram, restrained, precise, flat, no gradients, no texture, no shadow, no 3D, no lettering, no IBM wordmark. Palette: opaque white with a small IBM blue #78a9ff accent, designed to contrast on a #161616 header. Actual transparent background with alpha, NOT a black rectangle or checkerboard artwork. Compact centered square image with emblem occupying approximately 88% of canvas height, minimal surrounding transparent margins. Render one finished emblem only.
